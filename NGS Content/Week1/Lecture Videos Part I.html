Note: the Illumina family of sequencers has expanded dramatically since Video 1 was recorded. Of note are the NextSeq 2000, NovaSeq 6000, and NovaSeq X. The NovaSeq is now one of the main workhorses in the sequencing industry today. It can produce up 6000 Gb of sequence in one run. Since the human genome is 3 Gb, that means you could sequence 2000 human genomes at 1X coverage, or 100 genomes at 20X coverage in one sequencing run (*coverage will be discussed in detail later in the course).
